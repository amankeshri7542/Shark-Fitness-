
## Summary

Completes the engagement and operations modules the PRD navigation lists but the product lacked:

- **One NotificationService** for in-app, push, email, SMS and WhatsApp. Every channel defaults to a `log` driver that records "logged, not delivered"; real drivers (Resend, MSG91 Flow, Twilio, WhatsApp Cloud API with template names) are chosen by env and only ever tested against local fake servers. Every attempt is a `notification_deliveries` row. Consent is checked by purpose when a message is queued and again just before sending. Quiet hours (branch time) defer messages, dedupe keys stop repeats, a missing template variable stops the send, and retries are bounded. Automations, dunning and broadcasts use it, and the Automations screen shows each channel's real state.
- **Member messaging**: members start threads with their coach or the branch desk, with the reply window and outside-hours note shown first. They can attach photos and PDFs through a MediaStorage seam, served by signed, re-checked URLs with GPS metadata stripped. Staff↔minor threads are refused unless the gym enables them. A new staff **Inbox** has assignment, read state, replies and private notes. Queued messages now survive leaving the thread.
- **Community**: comments, report with a reason, mute, block (also on shared challenge boards), rate limits, approval mode, posting bans, and no public name for minors. A new **Community** console module has a reports queue with context, hide/remove/restore/keep with required reasons (audited, author told in-app), an approval queue and bans.
- **Tasks**: generated from operational alerts with idempotent keys, plus manual tasks. Each has an assignee, due date, priority, status, link and a required outcome. "Create task" is on Command Center alerts, member and lead pages and in the palette. The status strip shows My tasks and the alert-born work still open.
- **Nutrition & Habits** coach view: assigned members only, flagged check-ins first, habit templates, replies. Members who opted out are excluded. Members send the weekly check-in from the Habits screen (added in review; the API existed but no member screen called it).
- **Marketing**: one-off broadcasts to a segment. Preview and dry run use the same checks as the send, so exclusion counts and quiet-hour holds match what actually goes. Cost is recipients × the gym's rate. Owner approval with step-up is required above a threshold, followed by a delivery report. An approval covers the audience it saw; a send that has grown past it needs approving again.
- **Quick add** for walk-ins with duplicate detection (links to the existing member) and activation.
- **Command palette**: an ARIA combobox that searches members, leads, invoices, classes and staff on the server (permission- and scope-aware), lists modules, keeps recents, and runs actions: Add member, Record payment, Check in member, Create class and New task.
- `navFor` is permission-aware for the new modules. Reception's rail is now 7 modules (tested); everything else stays one ⌘K away.

## Decisions and assumptions

**Navigation**
- Reception's rail had 8 modules and the spec requires ≤ 7, so the rail is now curated: Command, Inbox, Members, Floor, Billing, Schedule, Leads. Store, Support and Tasks stay one ⌘K away, and Tasks is in the status strip.
- The Inbox is its own module rather than a Support tab, because coaches must answer threads without seeing the support queue.

**Notification consent**
- *Transactional*: always sent.
- *Operational* and *coaching*: on until the member switches them off (new consent switches).
- *Promotional*: email, SMS and WhatsApp need explicit per-channel consent. Automations keep their previous marketing-consent behaviour.
- Quiet hours do not apply to in-app or transactional messages.

**Channels and drivers**
- Automations still cannot go live on a log-only channel (the existing "Provider required" gate is kept). With a real driver they hand off to the service.
- SMTP email is **not implemented**: Resend only. SMTP is not HTTP and would need another dependency. Recorded as a follow-up.
- MSG91 needs a DLT flow id, and WhatsApp needs an approved template name. A message without one is refused with the reason, never sent untemplated.

**Media**
- P05's storage is not on this branch, so there is a minimal `MediaStorage` port (local disk) and an HMAC signer. Production uploads stay off until `SHARK_MEDIA_DIR` is set.
- Client-supplied attachment URLs are always refused. The older provider-truthfulness test was split so it still proves the "no storage" truth with storage switched off.

**Community**
- Hide is reversible and visible to the author; remove leaves a tombstone. Every decision needs a reason that is shown to the author.
- Mute is one-way; block is two-way.

**Tasks**
- One task per missed lead follow-up, failed payment, open safety work order and stale waitlist offer, and one per at-risk member per month.
- Tasks are not auto-closed when the cause resolves; an outcome is required to finish.

**Broadcasts**
- Recipients in quiet hours are counted as held, not excluded.
- Defaults: approval above 200 recipients; email ₹0.10, SMS ₹0.25, WhatsApp ₹0.80 per message.
- Only `{{firstName}}` is supported in the message body.
- Approval requires owner step-up, and covers the audience as approved (re-approval if it grows).
- Raising the approval threshold or allowing staff↔minor messages also requires step-up (`settings.engagement`).

**Other**
- Quick add names an existing member only when that member is in the caller's scope.
- In the palette, typed commands rank above search hits.

**Dependency**: `@axe-core/playwright` 4.10.2 (e2e dev only), needed for the AC8 accessibility check.

## Independent review

An independent senior review ran after the implementation. The full write-up is `phases/P07-review.md`. **Verdict: ready for PR, with known minor issues.**

**Fixed: 10 major findings.** Each has a regression test, committed as `fix:` commits after `0621707`.
- **Inbox:**
  - A coach kept reading and replying to a member's coach threads after the member changed coach. Threads now follow the new coach.
  - Any `inbox.view` user could hand a desk thread to a trainer. The assignee's role is now checked.
- **Broadcasts:**
  - An owner's approval had no size bound. It now covers the audience the owner saw; a send that has grown past it needs approving again.
  - The console's idempotent send rolled back the "awaiting approval" state, so the owner never saw Approve. The hold is now recorded before that transaction.
- **Settings:** the approval threshold could be raised, and staff↔minor messaging switched on, without step-up. Both now need step-up.
- **Palette:** recents were shared across users and tenants on the same browser. They are now per person and cleared at sign-out.
- **Tasks:** generated tasks could be assigned to deactivated or out-of-branch staff. Such tasks now wait unassigned in the open queue.
- **Community:** the feed's comment count revealed that someone you blocked was still commenting. The count now matches what the reader sees.
- **Coaching:**
  - Re-sending a check-in cleared the coach's safety flag and lost the member's words. A flagged week now stays as written.
  - Members had no screen to send the weekly check-in. The Habits screen now has one, with component tests and a production-build check.

**Fixed: 8 minor findings.**
- The palette highlight now stays on the chosen item when results arrive.
- Only managers can unassign a task.
- Restore no longer bypasses the approval queue.
- Bulk messaging leaves out minors while staff↔minor messages are off.
- WebP EXIF/GPS metadata is stripped.
- The quick-add duplicate check is rate-limited.
- Support-ticket threads refuse attachments that staff could never open.
- Minor-rule test coverage is extended.

**Remaining (minor, not fixed):**
- The Inbox list truncates at 200 before role filtering.
- Unsent uploads are never cleaned up.
- Staff search caps at 12 before branch filtering.
- Branch pickers list branches outside the active one.
- Two task-key edge cases: a stale lead never regenerates, and at-risk tasks repeat monthly.
- Report does not re-check feed visibility.
- The feed limit is applied before block and mute filters (an older pattern).
- Approvals do not expire.
