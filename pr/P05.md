# P05: QR check-in, waitlists that hold seats, credit packs, private progress photos and freestyle workouts

## Summary

This turns back on, safely, the member features that stabilisation had switched off, and adds the ones that were missing:

- **Entry pass and reception scanning.** The member's pass is now a real rotating QR code, single use and changing every 30 seconds. Reception scans it on the Floor with the camera, a USB scanner or a typed code. The scan goes through the same decision the door uses.
- **Waitlists that hold the seat.** When a seat frees up, the first eligible member is offered it, and nobody else can book it while the offer stands. The database enforces this too.
- **Credit packs.** Packs can be sold at the desk or bought in the app. Credits are allocated in the same transaction as the payment that completes the invoice, used earliest expiry first, and refunded under decision 11.
- **Private media storage and progress photos.** Photos need consent, have their metadata stripped and are served only through signed links. They can be shared with the assigned coach only. Withdrawing consent hides them from the coach at once and deletes them after the retention period. Backups now include media.
- **"Log a workout of your own"** from the exercise library, offline included. It uses the same logger, outbox, PR detection, XP rules and Summary as a coached session.

Everything is backed by integration, component and Playwright tests. All gates pass (numbers below).

## What changed (by app)

### API (`apps/api`)
- **Pass and scan.**
  - `GET /v1/member/pass` returns the pass state (active, grace, frozen, expired, suspended, cancelled, pending, none) with a plain notice, the branches the member may use (`?branchId`), and a short run of 30-second single-use codes, sent `no-store`.
  - `POST /v1/admin/attendance/scan` runs `scanSignedPass` for a member of staff. It is tenant-bound (`denied_wrong_tenant`) and tells an expired code (`denied_token_expired`) apart from a forged one. A member already inside gets "Already checked in" with no second visit row. Every outcome is audited, the call is idempotent, and it emits occupancy. The override is offered only with `attendance.override`.
  - `Permissions-Policy: camera=(self)` is sent on `/admin/*` documents only.
- **Waitlist** (`services/waitlist.ts`; `WAITLIST_AVAILABLE` and the two disabled promotion copies are removed).
  - A seat freed by a cancellation, a desk release, a capacity increase, or a declined or lapsed offer goes to the first eligible WAITING member, and the hold counts in `booked`.
  - The offer lasts until the earlier of 30 minutes or 60 minutes before class. Both values are branch-overridable policies.
  - Accepting runs the normal claim (membership, branch, window and credits).
  - Lapsed offers pass on through `expire-waitlist-offers`.
  - Ineligible members are skipped with the reason audited, using a new `skipped` state.
  - A capacity cut below the people booked cancels nobody. Offers that no longer fit are withdrawn, and the member keeps their place. The cut also raises an `overbooked_class` dashboard alert and an audit row.
  - Database triggers count live bookings plus live offers against capacity. The `booked` guard now refuses increases only.
- **Credit packs** (`services/credit-ledger.ts`, `services/credit-packs.ts`, pure lots in `@shark/domain`).
  - New endpoints: `POST /v1/member/billing/credit-packs`, `POST /v1/admin/billing/members/:id/credit-packs`, `POST /v1/admin/billing/credit-packs/:id/refund`, and `POST /v1/admin/billing/members/:id/pt-sessions`.
  - Allocation happens inside `settleInvoicePayment`.
  - Spending is FIFO by lot. A credit restored on a timely cancel goes back to its own lot, so it keeps that lot's expiry.
  - A pack refund revokes credits atomically, and a failed Razorpay refund puts them back.
  - The `credits` table is append-only (triggers).
  - The credit read now includes lots, packs with a live refund quote, and the packs on sale. Packs and offers are hidden from roles without `billing.view`.
- **Media.**
  - `MediaStorage` port with a local-disk adapter (`SHARK_MEDIA_DIR`), checked at boot to sit outside every web build.
  - `POST /v1/member/media/uploads`: limited to 10 MB (413). The type is read from the bytes (422 otherwise). JPEG, PNG and WebP metadata is stripped in pure TypeScript, images over 4096 px are refused, and each tenant has a quota (429).
  - `GET /v1/files/:id`: a link signed for one viewer, valid for 5 minutes. A forged or expired link gets 403. The server re-checks access when the file is requested and returns 404 if the viewer may not see it.
  - Deleting keeps a tombstone row.
- **Progress photos.**
  - Consent is at version 2.0 and is recorded on each photo.
  - Visibility is private or the assigned coach only.
  - Withdrawing consent schedules deletion (`photoRetentionDaysAfterWithdrawal`, default 30), and a new `purge-withdrawn-photos` job carries it out.
  - The privacy export lists photos; erasure destroys them.
- **Training.** `GET /v1/member/training/exercises` returns the library, with each exercise's muscles, rest and the member's last working set.
- One migration: `0019_p05_member_core`.

## Decisions and assumptions

The client could not be asked; these follow PHASE-PLAN decisions 11 and 12 and the PRDs. All of them are recorded in `phases/P05-notes.md` (D1–D22).

- **Offer timing:** the earlier of 30 minutes or 60 minutes before class. If less than a minute would remain, no offer is made and the seat is open to anyone.
- **No-shows** are marked after class starts, which is always past the cut-off, so they create no offer. Releasing a booking before class does.
- **Late cancellation and no-show** forfeit the credit for every pack. No per-product override was added.
- **Refunds:**
  - Wholly unused: everything paid.
  - Partly used: unused credits × floor(price ÷ units), with a required reason.
  - Expired: refused.
  - A pack paid in several parts can't be refunded through this flow. It is refused with a plain message; this is rare.
- **PT credits** are spent by a staff "record a PT session" action. There is no PT booking flow in the product to hook into.
- **Media signing key** is derived from `SHARK_PASS_SECRET`, so no new required secret.
- **No `sharp`:** images are re-encoded on the phone and metadata is stripped on the server; anything over 4096 px is refused.
- **Uploads in production** stay off until `SHARK_MEDIA_DIR` is set.
- **Only the assigned coach** sees shared photos. Owners and managers do not, and an unassigned trainer gets 404.
- **Coaches** see credit balances but not pack prices or refunds.
- **Changed tests:** some existing tests asserted the old disabled behaviour (capacity-cut refusal, "no waitlist offer", credit sales unavailable, PT unavailable). They now assert the decided behaviour; each one is listed in the notes.

## Known limitations / follow-ups

- Push notifications for waitlist offers wait for P04's channel. They are in-app now. The hook point is `notifyMember()` in `services/waitlist.ts`.
- Member UI moves under `/app/` with P04 (files listed in the notes).
- **Scanning is online-only, like the door:** with the API unreachable, nobody is admitted by scan. Reception can still check members in by name or member number.
- No object-storage adapter yet; `SHARK_MEDIA_BUCKET` is still refused. Media lives on the API host's disk and in its backups.
- **No PT booking flow:** a PT credit is spent by recording a delivered session.
- A pack paid in several parts must be refunded by agreement outside the pack refund.
- **e2e and the sign-in limit:** the suite is close to the API's sign-in limit (10 per minute per IP), so new specs should reuse sessions. Specs P08 should add are listed in the notes.
- **To check on a real phone:** the offline freestyle flow, in a basement with a real service worker (the automated phone test passes), and BarcodeDetector camera scanning on reception hardware (the camera path is component-tested, and denied or missing cameras are tested end to end).
