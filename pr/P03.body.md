
## Summary

Shark Fitness now has a fast, SEO-complete public website at `/` (Astro, static): Home, Plans & pricing, Classes & timetable, Coaches, Locations (index + one page per branch), About, Free trial, Contact, FAQ, Privacy notice and Terms (both drafts for counsel), and a 404. Its free-trial and contact forms create real, deduplicated leads through a new rate-limited public API (`/v1/public/:tenantSlug`), with consent stored against the notice version and QR/UTM campaign attribution. Staff see website leads in the console with their campaign and trial request, and confirm trials from the lead. The member app moved to `/app/` with forwards for old links, old installs and the old root service worker. Lighthouse (mobile, simulated) scores 99–100 in every category on Home, Plans and Free trial.

## What changed

### Public website (`apps/website`, new)
- Astro 7.4.13 static site, TypeScript strict, linted (`.astro` included). Sonar design from `@shark/design-tokens`: abyss canvas, one cyan accent, Anton/Archivo/Archivo Narrow, zero radius, hairline seams, notched CTAs, contour graphic; light theme from `prefers-color-scheme`.
- Content: `src/content/snapshot.json` (branches, hours, holidays, published plans with GST-inclusive prices, weekly timetable) generated from the public API by `pnpm -F @shark/website sync-content -- --api <url>`; editorial content typed in `src/content/site.ts`.
- Live progressive enhancement: plan prices refresh from `GET /site`; Classes shows "This week, with spaces" from `GET /classes`; the trial form lists the next seven days' classes for the chosen branch (full classes disabled).
- Forms: native controls, keyboard-only friendly; client validation mirrors the API (phone rule unit-tested for parity), an `aria-live` summary, per-field errors via `aria-describedby`/`aria-invalid`, focus moves to the first invalid field; API 422 field paths mapped back; `Idempotency-Key`; honeypot; form-start time; UTM/referral captured for the visit from any landing page.
- SEO: unique titles/descriptions, canonical from `SITE_URL`, Open Graph/Twitter with a generated 1200×630 `og.png`, JSON-LD (`Organization`, `ExerciseGym` per branch with address/geo/opening hours/telephone, `OfferCatalog`/`Offer` with tax included (`valueAddedTaxIncluded`, i.e. GST), `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`), `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`; whole site `noindex` unless `SITE_INDEXABLE=true`; 404 always `noindex`.
- Performance: no framework runtime, inlined CSS, self-hosted subset WOFF2 with preload and `font-display: swap`, Brotli/gzip precompressed at build. JS: ~1–2 KB gzip on content pages, ~4 KB on the forms. No cookies, no third-party requests (optional analytics placeholder off by default).
- `/sw.js`: self-unregistering worker that retires the member app's old root-scoped worker; every page also unregisters any `/`-scoped worker. `/` forwards `#activationId=`/`#recoveryId=` fragments and standalone launches to `/app/`.

### API (`apps/api`)

## Decisions and assumptions
- **Astro over Next.js** (plan decision 4): static, near-zero JS, no extra server. Dependencies added only as dev dependencies: `astro`, `@astrojs/check`, `eslint-plugin-astro`/`astro-eslint-parser`, `@fontsource/*` (self-hosted fonts), `playwright-core` (OG image; already in the lockfile).
- **Prices are GST-inclusive and exact** (`₹3,538.82` for Depot Monthly): catalogue prices are pre-tax and invoices add 18%; a rounded figure would never match an invoice.
- **"Buy" from the website**: prospects cannot sign in to the member app (staff create members), so the primary plan action is the free week (`/free-trial?plan=<id>`, recorded as interest and expected value) and the member action is `/app/billing?plan=<id>` (P02's plan purchase).
- **Same 202 for every accepted or dropped submission**, so the form cannot reveal whether a number belongs to a member.
- **Dedupe**: one lead per person per gym; first-touch attribution kept; later campaigns noted; lost leads reopened; converted members linked via their lead (or a `won` lead linked to the member).
- **Lead SLA**: the existing mechanism (`nextActionAt` → overdue chip/count) is used; website leads get a deadline two hours after the branch next opens.
- **Confirm trial** works from any open pre-trial stage (the confirming call is also the contact) and never books a seat.
- **Range on `/classes` is clamped, not refused**; the effective range is returned.
- **Clock skew**: a negative fill time (visitor clock ahead) is not treated as a bot.
- **Legacy paths**: in addition to `/`'s fragment forwarding, old member routes redirect to `/app/…`, because activation links issued before the move were `/sign-in#…`.
- **Lighthouse method**: mobile, simulated throttling, all categories (the spec's `--preset=perf` would skip A11y/BP/SEO), against an `SITE_INDEXABLE=true` build on port 18404 (8788 is shared here).
- **Copy** is written for this gym from the seeded facts; coach credentials, postcodes, coordinates, transport notes and the founding year are placeholders the client must confirm. Privacy notice and terms are marked draft for counsel.

## Independent review

An independent reviewer read the diff, re-ran every check on the final tree and fixed six defects, each with a test that fails without the fix (full write-up: `phases/P03-review.md`). **Verdict: ready for PR, with the known minor issues below.**

Fixed:
- **Major: a repeat enquiry lost its new contact details.** A submission matched to an existing lead or member by phone *or* email kept only the stored name, phone and email, so a returning visitor's new number was recorded nowhere. Differing details now head the activity note (`41af417`).
- **Major: the legacy service-worker spec was vacuous.** It passed even with a no-op `/sw.js`. A new spec has an old worker serve its cached shell at `/` and proves the website's `/sw.js` retires it, on desktop and mobile (`bb8ec28`).
- **Minor:** `GET /classes` with an impossible date returned 500; it now returns 422 with a field path (`9c428c6`). The built-site SEO and JS-budget checks silently skipped in CI; they now run after the build and fail without one (`3ca642d`). `/app/manifest.webmanifest` was served as `application/octet-stream` (`75348e0`). The home page claimed 5 am–11 pm every day; weekends are 6 am–10 pm (`4fee7c8`).

Known minor issues, not fixed:
- The form keeps its Idempotency-Key until it gets a 422. If the response to a successful submission is lost and the visitor edits the form and resends, every retry gets a 409 until the page is reloaded. The enquiry itself is already stored.
- Audit rows for website submissions use the existing system-context pattern (`platform_admin` role, actor "Website form").
- A visitor who knows only a lead's phone number can add an email to that lead if it has none.
- `pnpm -F @shark/api test -- public` runs the whole API suite: the trailing filter is not applied.
