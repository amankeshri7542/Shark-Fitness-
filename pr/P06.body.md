
## Summary

Brings staff and admin security to the PRD 04 baseline:

- **Two-step sign-in (TOTP)**, mandatory for owners, regional managers and platform staff, optional per gym for all staff, never for members. QR + setup-key enrolment, ten hashed recovery codes, replay protection, lockout after five wrong codes, owner-assisted and platform-support resets. Secrets AES-256-GCM-encrypted with a new required production key, `SHARK_MFA_ENCRYPTION_KEY`.
- **Step-up re-authentication**: password (+ TOTP) within the last 5 minutes for refunds, write-offs, staff role/branch changes, exports, privacy erasure/approval, impersonation, MFA changes and webhook secrets. 401 `STEP_UP_REQUIRED`; the console shows one dialog and retries the original request with the same idempotency key.
- **Server-enforced sessions**: staff idle timeout on real activity (gym-set 15–240 min, default 30), 12-hour absolute limit; members 30-day sliding. Console: "Session expired" dialog that returns to the same URL, idle warning two minutes ahead, and "Your account" with Your devices (revoke one, sign out everywhere else).
- **Enforced Content-Security-Policy** on every surface from one config module, no `'unsafe-inline'`, fonts self-hosted, `/v1/csp-reports` intake. Every E2E spec fails on any CSP violation.
- **Outbound webhooks** (Settings → Integrations): signed (`Shark-Signature: t=,v1=`), driven from the outbox, retried with backoff for up to 24 h, auto-disabled after 15 consecutive failed attempts with an owner alert, delivery log, replay, test send, 24 h dual-signing on rotation, and SSRF defences.
- Removed the unreachable `POST /auth/otp/verify` in `auth.ts`; verified and tested `/platform/impersonate/end` authorization.

## Decisions and assumptions

- **TOTP now, passkeys later** (ADR-004): recovery and shared-desk device management need product decisions; the partial-session and step-up machinery is factor-agnostic.
- **Partial sessions** instead of a separate MFA token; the token is replaced when MFA completes.
- **Each TOTP code is accepted once** (not in the spec; NIST 800-63B). Test helpers reset the marker (API) or wait for the next step (e2e); replay refusal itself is tested.
- **Successful code sign-ins are logged, not audited**, like password sign-ins; enrol, disable, lockout, recovery-code use, regeneration and resets are audited.
- **"Flagged" demo owners** need no column: the gate is derived from role or the gym setting, so a fresh demo owner meets the enrolment gate on first sign-in.
- **Idle means no person at the keyboard**: background reads don't extend a staff session; writes and an input-driven heartbeat do.
- **Step-up scope** also covers POS returns, staff creation, privacy-export reads, security settings and webhook URL changes (each is a high-risk act under PRD 01 §2.4's intent). Name/phone edits to staff do not ask.
- **CSP per surface by path**; Razorpay only on the member surface; `/` is the member app until P03 (`ROOT_SURFACE`). Razorpay publishes no CSP allowlist — origins must be confirmed by P02's sandbox E2E.
- **Self-hosted fonts** were required for `font-src 'self'`/`style-src 'self'`. Member precache grows 589 → 826 KiB.
- **Webhooks**: N = 15 consecutive failures; backoff 1 m → 12 h within 24 h; no contact details in payloads; create/URL change/secret reveal/rotate behind step-up; test sends are one attempt and don't count towards disabling.
- **New dependencies**: `otpauth` 9.6.0 (TOTP), `uqr` 0.1.3 (QR encoding, zero deps), `@fontsource/anton|archivo|archivo-narrow` 5.3.0 (self-hosted fonts).
